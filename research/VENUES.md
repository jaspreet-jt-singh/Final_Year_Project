## Selection gate

First obtain supervisor agreement on the contribution and publication requirements (including whether a conference is preferred and required indexing). Then compare recent accepted papers in the two candidate journals, check current author instructions, and choose one. If a conference is preferred, shortlist an upcoming official call only after the experiment schedule is credible; do not invent a deadline or pay registration to force an unfinished submission. Do not submit substantially overlapping manuscripts simultaneously.

## Initial related-work boundary

- [Nutrition5k (CVPR 2021)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Thames_Nutrition5k_Towards_Automatic_Nutritional_Understanding_of_Generic_Food_CVPR_2021_paper.html) includes measured dish/component information and nutrition annotations. The present app has user-estimated grams and database matches, not equivalent portion/meal ground truth.
- [Decomposing Food Images for Better Nutrition Analysis (CVPR Workshops 2025)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/MTF/html/Khlaisamniang_Decomposing_Food_Images_for_Better_Nutrition_Analysis_A_Nutritionist-Inspired_Two-Step_CVPRW_2025_paper.html) already evaluates a staged multimodal nutrition pipeline. A staged detector-plus-recommendation architecture alone is not a defensible novelty claim.
- Existing manuscript references to IndianFoodNet, FKG.in and diet-assistant systems require a full methods comparison. This initial check is not an exhaustive novelty review and does not establish that conservative mapping is new.
